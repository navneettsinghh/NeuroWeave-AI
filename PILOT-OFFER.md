# CONVERTIQ Pilot Offer

## AI Lead Follow-Up Pilot

This offer is a starting hypothesis for validation, not a guaranteed market price.

### Suggested pricing

- Setup: ₹15,000–₹25,000
- Monthly support/optimization: ₹3,000–₹5,000

## Included in the pilot

- Lead intake configuration for the selected source(s)
- Real-estate qualification flow customization
- Business-specific messaging and tone
- Lead prioritization using transparent HOT/WARM/COLD rules
- Agent alerts for high-intent enquiries
- Basic pipeline for new, contacted, qualified, hot, booked, and lost leads
- Demo/training walkthrough for the sales team
- Setup support and early workflow adjustments

## Not included in the first pilot

- Full SaaS account system
- Billing/subscription management
- Multi-tenancy
- Production WhatsApp API integration without approved provider setup
- Custom mobile app
- Advanced analytics warehouse
- Voice AI
- Instagram, SMS, or email automation
- Complex drag-and-drop automation builder

## Pilot success criteria

The pilot should validate whether the business values:

1. Faster first response to property enquiries
2. More consistent qualification
3. Clearer lead priority for agents
4. Better visibility into at-risk leads
5. A practical handoff from AI response to human follow-up

## Recommended pilot scope

Start with one business location/team, one or two lead sources, one qualification workflow, and one sales manager reviewing results weekly.
