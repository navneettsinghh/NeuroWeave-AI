# CONVERTIQ Sales Copy

## One-line pitch

CONVERTIQ helps real-estate teams respond instantly, qualify property buyers, prioritize hot enquiries, and know who to contact next.

## 30-second pitch

Real-estate teams often lose opportunities because WhatsApp enquiries are answered late, follow-up is inconsistent, and agents do not always know which leads are most urgent. CONVERTIQ acts as an AI-assisted follow-up layer: it responds instantly, asks qualification questions, identifies HOT/WARM/COLD priority, and recommends the next agent action while keeping human takeover available.

## 60-second pitch

CONVERTIQ is built for real-estate agents and property consultants who already receive leads from ads, portals, websites, or WhatsApp. Instead of letting every enquiry sit in the same inbox, CONVERTIQ replies quickly, captures budget, location, property type, and buying timeline, then highlights the leads that deserve immediate attention. The goal of the first pilot is not to replace your CRM or your sales team. It is to validate whether faster first response, structured qualification, and clear agent recommendations improve your follow-up workflow.

## Common objections

### “We already use WhatsApp.”

CONVERTIQ is not simply another messaging interface. WhatsApp is the channel; CONVERTIQ is the qualification and prioritization workflow on top of that channel. It helps identify which conversations need agent attention now.

### “My team already follows up.”

That is good. CONVERTIQ is designed to make follow-up more consistent by highlighting missed, at-risk, and high-priority opportunities. It helps managers see where follow-up discipline may be breaking down.

### “Will AI replace my agents?”

No. The workflow is AI-assisted, not agent-replacing. AI handles repetitive first response and qualification. Human takeover is always available for important buyer conversations.

### “Can this work with our current lead sources?”

The paid pilot can be configured around the lead sources you already use where practical. The demo intentionally avoids promising a specific production integration until your lead sources and WhatsApp setup are reviewed.

### “Is this a full CRM?”

Not initially. The pilot focuses on first response, qualification, priority, agent alerts, and a basic pipeline. A full CRM should only be added if the pilot proves there is enough value.
