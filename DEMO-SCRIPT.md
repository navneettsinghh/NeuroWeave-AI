# CONVERTIQ 90-Second Demo Script

Use this script to show the demo to a real-estate business owner or sales manager without technical explanation.

## 0–10 sec — Problem

“Most real-estate teams already pay for enquiries through ads, portals, referrals, and WhatsApp. The problem is not only getting leads — it is responding fast, qualifying them, and knowing which ones need attention first.”

## 10–25 sec — Lead arrives

Click **Experience Live Demo** or **Demo Mode**.

“Here is a realistic buyer enquiry: Rahul Sharma is looking for a 3 BHK apartment near City Center with an ₹80 lakh budget and a purchase timeline within 30 days.”

## 25–40 sec — AI responds and qualifies

Point to the WhatsApp-style conversation.

“CONVERTIQ responds instantly, asks the right qualification questions, and captures budget, location, property type, and timeline before an agent spends time manually chasing details.”

## 40–55 sec — Lead becomes HOT

Point to the progress indicator, HOT lead alert, and lead priority.

“Because Rahul has a specific budget, location, property requirement, and timeline under 30 days, CONVERTIQ marks him as a HOT opportunity.”

## 55–70 sec — Agent receives recommendation

Point to **AI Recommended Actions** and the lead detail panel.

“The system does not just chat. It tells the sales team what to do next: contact this lead within 5 minutes and share 2–3 matching properties.”

## 70–80 sec — Human takeover

Click **Human Takeover**.

“AI handles repetitive first response. Your agent can take over any important conversation at any time.”

## 80–90 sec — Appointment + CTA

Click **Mark Booked**, then scroll to **Request a Pilot**.

“This is the outcome we want to validate in a paid pilot: faster response, clearer priority, better follow-up discipline, and more qualified conversations moving toward appointments.”
